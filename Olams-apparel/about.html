About Olams Apparel

We make available luxury, and we do it with decency.

Our story

Olam's Apparel started in 2020 in a single room at Solomon Okonkwo, Egbeda, Lagos. Its founder, Mubarak Olodan, had a sewing machine, a few rolls of fabric and a clear belief that good clothing should make a man feel dignified.

There was no shop and no showroom. Every outfit was measured, cut and finished by hand in that room, and every customer was treated like family. People came back, and they told their friends.

Today Olam's Apparel makes agbada, caftans, casuals and two-piece sets, including designs inspired by Senegal, for customers in Lagos and around the world. The room in Egbeda is where it began, and the same care goes into every piece.
